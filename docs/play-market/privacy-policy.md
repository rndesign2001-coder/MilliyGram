# MilliyGram — Maxfiylik siyosati / Privacy Policy

_Oxirgi yangilanish / Last updated: 2026-09-29_

## O'zbekcha

**MilliyGram** — Telegram'ning ochiq manba kodi (GNU GPL v2) asosida qurilgan norasmiy Telegram klienti. Ilova Telegram FZ-LLC tomonidan ishlab chiqilmagan.

**Qanday ma'lumotlar ishlatiladi**
- Xabarlar, kontaktlar, media va akkaunt ma'lumotlari faqat Telegram serverlari bilan almashiladi — xuddi rasmiy Telegram ilovasidagidek. MilliyGram ishlab chiquvchilari bu ma'lumotlarni ko'rmaydi, yig'maydi va saqlamaydi.
- MilliyGram'ning qo'shimcha funksiyalari (chat qulfi, yashirin bo'lim, obunachilar kundaligi, avto-o'chirish ro'yxati, sozlamalar) faqat telefoningiz xotirasida saqlanadi va hech qayerga yuborilmaydi.
- **Joylashuv**: namoz vaqti, ob-havo va qibla uchun tanlagan tumaningiz koordinatasi ishlatiladi. GPS faqat siz "GPS bilan aniqlash"ni yoqsangiz ishlatiladi va natija faqat telefonda saqlanadi.
- **Ob-havo**: tuman koordinatasi (shaxsingizsiz) Open-Meteo xizmatiga (open-meteo.com) yuboriladi.
- **Tarjima**: siz tarjima qilishni tanlagan matn Telegram tarjimoniga yoki Google Tarjimon'ga (translate.googleapis.com) yuboriladi.
- **Mikrofon** faqat ovozli xabar, qo'ng'iroq va "ovoz bilan yozish" uchun; **kamera** — surat/video uchun; **kontaktlar** — Telegram kontaktlarini sinxronlash uchun (Telegram'ning standart funksiyasi).
- Reklama, analitika yoki kuzatuv (tracking) SDK'lari ishlatilmaydi.

**Ma'lumotni o'chirish**: ilovani o'chirsangiz, MilliyGram sozlamalari ham o'chadi. Telegram akkauntingizni o'chirish: https://my.telegram.org/delete

**Bolalar**: ilova 13 yoshdan kichiklar uchun mo'ljallanmagan.

**Aloqa**: GitHub orqali — https://github.com/rndesign2001-coder/MilliyGram/issues

## English

**MilliyGram** is an unofficial Telegram client built on Telegram's open-source code (GNU GPL v2). It is not developed by Telegram FZ-LLC.

- Messages, contacts, media and account data are exchanged only with Telegram servers, exactly like the official app. The MilliyGram developers do not see, collect or store this data.
- MilliyGram's extra features (chat lock, hidden section, subscriber diary, auto-delete queue, settings) are stored only on your device and are never uploaded.
- **Location**: the coordinates of the district you choose are used for prayer times, weather and Qibla direction. GPS is used only if you enable "GPS" and the result stays on the device.
- **Weather**: district coordinates (not linked to you) are sent to Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com).
- **Translation**: text you choose to translate is sent to Telegram's translator or Google Translate (translate.googleapis.com).
- **Microphone** is used for voice messages, calls and voice typing; **camera** for photos/videos; **contacts** for Telegram contact sync (standard Telegram feature).
- No advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs are used.

**Data deletion**: uninstalling the app removes all MilliyGram settings. To delete your Telegram account: https://my.telegram.org/delete

**Children**: the app is not intended for children under 13.
